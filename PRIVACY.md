# Privacy policy

VRCFaceTracking does not collect, transmit or store any personal data. There is no crash reporting or analytics service in the application.

Diagnostic logs are written only to your own machine, under `%LocalAppData%\VRCFaceTracking\logs`, and are never sent anywhere. You can delete them at any time.

The application talks to VRChat over OSC on your local network. The module registry downloads module packages from GitHub when you install or update a module. Apart from those two, the application itself doesn't make network connections. Installed tracking modules may behave differently and have their own policies.

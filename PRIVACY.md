# Privacy Policy

VRCFaceTracking does not collect, transmit or store any personal data. There is no crash reporting or
analytics service in the application.

Diagnostic logs are written only to your own machine under `%LocalAppData%\VRCFaceTracking\logs` and
are never sent anywhere. You can delete them at any time.

The application talks to VRChat over OSC on your local network, and the module registry downloads
module packages from GitHub when you install or update a module. No other network traffic is made by
the application itself; installed tracking modules may have their own behaviour and policies.
